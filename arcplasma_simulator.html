Kinetic Vacuum Arc Dynamics & Nanosecond Quenching Theory

Physical mechanisms governing metal-vapor arc inception and counter-pulse plasma deionization

1. Cathode Spot Metal-Vapor Evaporation

Hard Vacuum

In hard lunar vacuum ($P \sim 10^{-12}\text{ Torr}$), gaseous Townsend breakdowns cannot occur due to the absence of neutral gas molecules (mean free path $\lambda_{\text{mfp}} > 10^4\text{ m}$). However, localized micrometeoroid impacts or micro-protrusion explosive field electron emission (Joule heating) trigger microscopic cathodic explosive evaporation, creating a localized metallic plasma sheath:

$$j_{\text{FN}} = A_{\text{FN}} \frac{\beta^2 E^2}{\phi} \exp\left( -B_{\text{FN}} \frac{\phi^{3/2}}{\beta E} v(y) \right)$$

When current density exceeds $j_{\text{crit}} \approx 10^{12}\text{ A/m}^2$, cathode spot micro-craters violently erupt, expelling metallic copper/aluminum vapor at supersonic velocity ($v_{\text{exp}} \sim 10^4\text{ m/s}$) with density:

$$n_v(x, t) = \frac{\gamma I_{\text{arc}}}{e Z_{\text{ion}} \pi r_{\text{spot}}^2} \exp\left( - \frac{x^2}{4 D t} \right)$$

$\gamma$: erosion coefficient ($\sim 40\text{ }\mu\text{g/C}$), $Z_{\text{ion}}$: mean ionization state (+1.8), $\beta$: micro-tip field enhancement factor.

2. $(Q, G)$ Phase-Space Observer Metric

Core Insight

Harmless electrostatic discharges (ESD) or glow-phase transients transfer charge $Q = \int I \mathrm{d}t$ with self-limiting conductance. In contrast, runaway vacuum vapor arcs undergo an explosive exponential growth in channel conductance $G(t) = I(t) / V_{\text{line}}(t)$:

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}G}{\mathrm{d}Q} = \frac{1}{I(t)} \frac{\mathrm{d}G}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{\sigma_{\text{plasma}} \cdot \mu_e \nu_{\text{ion}}}{V_{\text{line}}(t)} \cdot \exp\left( \frac{\alpha_{\text{ion}} \int I \mathrm{d}t}{Q_0} \right)$$

If the real-time observer evaluates:

$$\Phi(t) = \left. \frac{\partial G}{\partial Q} \right|_{\Delta t = 10\text{ ns}} > \Lambda_{\text{crit}} \quad \left( \approx 0.85\text{ mS}/\mu\text{C} \right)$$

The algorithm classifies an inevitable power-arc formation $40\text{--}80\text{ ns}$ *before* peak fault current is reached, preventing catastrophic busbar insulator melting.

Eliminates nuisance tripping caused by harmless cosmic-ray ionization cascades or charging dust impacts.

3. Forced Current Zero Injection (Counter-Pulse)

Nanosecond Quench

DC circuits lack natural zero-crossings. ArcPlasma utilizes a pre-charged Pulse-Forming Network (PFN) switched by an ultra-fast Gallium Nitride (GaN) / SiC MOSFET bridge that discharges an inverse counter-current $I_{\text{counter}}(t)$:

$$I_{\text{net}}(t) = I_{\text{fault}}(t) - I_{\text{PFN}} \sin(\omega_0 t) \exp(-\alpha t) = 0 \quad \text{at } t = t_{\text{zero}}$$

When $I_{\text{net}} \le 0$, cathode spot heating collapses instantly. Metallic plasma disperses into the ambient lunar vacuum at expansion velocity $u_{\text{plasma}} \approx 1.5 \times 10^4\text{ m/s}$. The dielectric recovery strength recovers according to:

$$V_{\text{breakdown}}(t) = V_{\infty} \left[ 1 - \exp\left( - \frac{t - t_{\text{zero}}}{\tau_{\text{deion}}} \right) \right], \quad \tau_{\text{deion}} \sim 120\text{ ns}$$

Complete deionization achieved in $< 300\text{ ns}$, safely below the thermal degradation limit of alumina standoff insulators.

4. Nanosecond FPGA & Rogowski Observer Circuit

Hardware Spec

Hardware implementation couples printed differential Rogowski coils ($B$-field) with capacitive voltage dividers ($E$-field) integrated directly into the insulator flange:

$$V_{\text{Rogowski}} = - M \frac{\mathrm{d}I}{\mathrm{d}t}, \quad Q(k) = \sum_{m=0}^k I(m) \Delta t, \quad G(k) = \frac{I(k)}{V(k)}$$
  • FPGA Engine: Radiation-hardened AMD/Xilinx Space-Grade Kintex UltraScale sampling at $500\text{ MSPS}$ (2 ns decision latency).
  • Solid-State Switch: 6.5 kV, 1.2 kA SiC Super-Junction modules with $< 15\text{ ns}$ turn-on jitter.
  • Energy Dissipation: High-energy metal oxide varistors (MOV) clamp inductive energy $E = \frac{1}{2} L_{\text{line}} I^2$.
